Two Decades of Surgical Excellence & Innovation

Dr. Mamoun Aldurra - Consultant Vascular Surgeon

With over two decades of experience as a Vascular Surgeon, Dr. Mamoun Aldurra is committed to advancing patient outcomes, limb preservation, and cutting-edge surgical innovations.

Initially specializing in General Surgery in Damascus with significant expertise in Laparoscopic Surgery, his commitment to advancing medical knowledge led him to pursue high-level fellowship training at the University of Lille 2 (France), focusing on Kidney and Liver Transplantation and earning a specialty certificate (AFS) in Vascular Surgery, alongside training at Centre Hospitalier de Beauvais in visceral, oncological, and laparoscopic surgery.

Passionate about enhancing patient outcomes, Dr. Aldurra developed specialized and precise surgical tools, several of which are patented internationally (WIPO & Turkey). He presented one of his major vascular innovations to Boston Scientific, receiving great admiration for its precision and tissue protection capabilities.

Vascular Retractor (Vascular Anastomosis Aiding Device)

VASCULAR ANASTOMOSIS AIDING DEVICE
Issued: Mar 4, 2010 WIPO / International PCT

A specialized vascular anastomosis aiding device composed of a plastic base with hooked legs made from flexible steel wire extending from it for safely engaging and pulling the vessel wall. Provides optimal exposure of the anastomosis site, prevents vessel wall trauma, and saves precious operative time.

Clinical Impact: Minimizes vessel trauma, saves time, presented to Boston Scientific.

Plumbing the Blood Vessels

RAPID EMERGENCY VASCULAR CONNECTION SYSTEM
Filed: Jan 10, 2020 Turkey Patent Office

A revolutionary method that shifts emergency vascular surgery from delicate traditional suture anastomosis to rapid modular tube assembling using specialized biomaterials. Designed to save limbs and lives in urgent trauma settings even when a vascular surgeon is unavailable, applicable by any general practitioner.

Clinical Impact: Prevents amputations in battlefield and civilian emergency trauma.
